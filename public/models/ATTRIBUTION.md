# Portrait study source

The portrait-study.glb geometry is the Lee Perry-Smith head scan, © I-R Entertainment Ltd., provided under [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).

Source GLB: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/dev/examples/models/gltf/LeePerrySmith

Author: Lee Perry-Smith / Infinite-Realities. Original model and licensing information: https://casual-effects.com/g3d/data10/research/model/lpshead/info.js

Archive credit: Morgan McGuire, Computer Graphics Archive, July 2017 (https://casual-effects.com/data).

Adaptations in this portfolio: scaled geometry, original material and lighting treatment, original jacket and hair, and procedural animation. This is an illustrative digital character, not a representation of Shaaz's actual face.
